Event-centered analysis

The analyzer extracts minimum voltage, voltage-dip intervals, recorded brownout events, shooter recovery intervals, and intake movement windows. Event windows bring neighboring samples into view.

Per-mechanism demand

Supply-current streams identify motor demand on the battery. PDH aggregate current and summed motor supply current remain distinct, while stator current describes torque-producing motor current.

Time-aware data handling

The parser accepts direct recorder names, sorts timestamps, and preserves missing samples. Timestamp gaps keep separate events separate, and duplicate timestamps are reported explicitly.

The recorder behind the analysis +

Shared context: mode, match time, shooting request, target mode, solver reason, feed blockers, manual override, and feed transition timestamps sit beside pose motion and actuator targets.

Electrical and device health: PDH voltage/current, roboRIO supply state, motor supply/stator currents, voltage, temperature, faults, resets, signal age, and CAN health describe the same operating interval.

Sampling architecture: configured motor electrical signals request 50 Hz, dedicated turret motion sampling runs at 100 Hz, slow health records at 10 Hz, and the power dashboard refreshes at 5 Hz. Battery and configuration labels identify the run.
